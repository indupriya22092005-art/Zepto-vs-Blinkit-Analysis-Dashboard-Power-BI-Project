# Grocery Store War: Zepto vs Blinkit | Power BI Dashboard

This project presents a comprehensive comparative analysis of two leading quick-commerce platforms, Zepto and Blinkit, using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and dashboards. The dataset consisted of more than 500 records containing information such as product categories, order values, payment modes, customer ratings, delivery times, cities, and purchase trends.

The project began with data cleaning and preprocessing in Excel, where missing values, inconsistencies, and formatting issues were addressed to ensure data quality. The cleaned dataset was then imported into Power BI, where relationships, measures, and calculated fields were created to support analytical reporting.

The dashboard provides a detailed comparison of Zepto and Blinkit across multiple business dimensions. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) such as Total Sales, Average Delivery Time, and Average Customer Rating were developed to provide a quick overview of platform performance. Interactive slicers were implemented for Brand, Quarter, Category, and Payment Mode, enabling users to dynamically filter and explore the data.

Several visualizations were incorporated to enhance decision-making. Monthly sales trends help identify seasonal demand patterns, while category-wise analysis highlights the best-performing product segments. Payment mode distribution charts reveal customer preferences among UPI, Card, and Cash on Delivery transactions. City-wise performance analysis provides insights into regional sales contributions and market penetration. Additionally, comparative metrics enable users to evaluate operational efficiency and customer satisfaction between the two platforms.

Through this project, I gained hands-on experience in data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, dashboard development, and data storytelling. The project demonstrates how Business Intelligence tools can convert raw data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making.
